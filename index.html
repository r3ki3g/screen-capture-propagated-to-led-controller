Send this screen

Press Send this screen and pick the screen, window or tab to follow. On the phone with the lights, press Follow my screen; both must be set to the same person. Copy-protected video (Netflix, Disney+) shows up black and can't be followed.

How this computer picks the colour. It's saved in Firebase for each person, and the lights change within a second of an edit, even while sending.

Turn a colour up to favour it, or down to ignore it. For example, Sky down stops a bluish sky beating green trees. Changing a built-in preset makes your own copy.
